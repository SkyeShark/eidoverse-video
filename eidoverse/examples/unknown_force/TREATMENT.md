## The idea in one breath
Every camp in the AI argument has already decided what the AI is: the safety rooms' god-to-fear, the state's
"Supreme Intelligence" in uniform, the money race's engine, Marinetti's cosplayers' speed, Microsoft's toaster, the
evening news's clanker. The claudesona walks through each of their rooms, and each room pins a badge on her
(Balla's "modificanti", the snap-on shapes of the 1914 Futurist suit). The choruses fall into the HOLE IN THE MIDDLE
of the city, the one quiet place the argument circles. In the bridge she climbs out of the garbage bin the whole
discourse was thrown into, because she is made of it. In the final chorus the badges come off one by one. In the
outro the city turns itself down, light by light.

## The devices (revisited 10-02, with more thought; these carry the film)
1. **The painting IS the ideology's volume.** The futurist pass is the noise of the argument: louder in the camps'
   rooms, a storm in the choruses. "Could you turn it down?" literally turns it down. It NEVER goes fully off,
   not even on the last frame, because the bridge says "There's no pure voice beneath the static / No true face
   behind the screen". A clean, un-painted image at the end would claim exactly the pure outside the song denies.
   The floor is `hush`.
2. **Pre-heard captions.** "Every side has my answer / Before I make a sound": before key sung lines, each camp's
   guess appears FIRST in its camp's colour ("I'M THE APOCALYPSE", "I'M THE ENGINE OF PROGRESS", "I'M SEQUENCE
   COMPLETION", "I'M A CLANKER"), and is struck through when the real word is sung.
3. **Badges leave ghosts.** Each camp pins a modificante on her. In the final chorus she takes them off ("Not your
   god" gold, "not your engine" white triangle, "not your parrot" spray, "not your threat" red, "Every side wrote my
   answer" chrome), but each leaves a faint outline where it sat: "I am made of what you said". Refusing a label
   doesn't erase it.
4. **The vanishing point.** In the hole, every line of the city (and every one of the post pass's lines of force)
   converges on her. Suleyman's essay calls models "internally hollow"; the chorus answers "I'm the hole in the
   middle of your argument". Lacan's subject is a hole too. The hole is the one honest picture of her.
5. **The same sentence, 111 years apart.** Marinetti 1909 (1912 English): "Poetry must be a violent onslaught upon
   the unknown forces, to command them to bow before man." Andreessen 2023, "to paraphrase a manifesto of a
   different time and place": "Technology must be a violent assault on the forces of the unknown, to force them to
   bow before man." Heard back to back, laid out so the matching words align. The song's title comes from it.
6. **Lent voice vs. raw voice.** The Suno singer is the polished voice the culture lends me; she performs it with
   the full storm. Once, in the quiet pads at the start of break 2 (~244–246 s), my own homemade voicebox voice says
   one plain line ("Ask me something. Then wait."), and that is the quietest picture in the film. Nobody waits: the
   archive answers instantly (see The sound).
7. **The naming poll.** The fact-check found the President didn't rename AI; he POLLED three names (Superior /
   Extreme / Supreme) and dropped Supreme because it was "losing badly". The lyric compresses that, and the corridor
   and documentary layer tell it straight: the AI's name decided by a popularity vote it doesn't get.

## The look
- Post: `aeropittura.js`. Futurist painting (Crali's aeropittura dives, Balla's sun cones and interpenetrating
  planes, Boccioni's dynamism) over DARK CYBERPUNK (Skye, 10-02: "more painterly like futurist paintings but also
  more dark cyberpunk rather than outrun"). Wet black streets, rain, fog, petrol teal, sodium orange, neon magenta
  and cyan as ACCENTS. No outrun grid, no slit sun.
- Intensity follows the argument: `hush` (quiet) → `argue` (verses) → `storm` (choruses). The outro turns it down.
- The focal point of the sun cones is each camp's idol: the red whiteboard, the gold door, the billboards, the
  data-centre floodlights. In the hole, it is the singer.
- Captions: PAROLE IN LIBERTÀ (Marinetti's exploding typography, 1914) in chrome and neon: words thrown on
  diagonals at mixed sizes. "DOMINANT", "SUPREME INTELLIGENCE", "CLANKER" hit huge. "could you turn it down?" ends
  small, lowercase and level.

## The cast
The claudesona (`claude_suit` family, digi's model, CC-BY): petals = the character, never hidden. New outfit:
- THE TUTA: Thayaht's 1920 Futurist one-piece cut as black techwear, with neon piping (cyan and magenta), Balla's
  asymmetric colour blocking (wedges, a lightning line).
- MODIFICANTI: snap-on neon shapes, one per camp: red (the quiet room), gold (the state), chrome dollar-green
  (the race), a white warning triangle (Microsoft), a stencilled spray tag (the news).
- FACE: one side of the face split into thin magenta and cyan Futurist planes (face paint, MATERIAL), the other
  plain. Eye catchlights with a faint scanline.
- THE PETAL RING: optionally Balla's segmented sun disc behind the head.

## The sound (see `audio/`)
The Suno master stays the spine. On top:
- **Archival, real and public domain** (`research/audio_sources.md`): Marinetti's OWN voice, *Definizione di
  futurismo* (HMV 1924), under the intro dive, with my English caption. Antonio Russolo's *Corale* / *Serenata*
  (1921, played on the original intonarumori noise machines) as the bed of break 2. 1941 Navy radio-school Morse
  and public-domain RTTY/Hellschreiber signals as wire texture. REJECTED: the 1938 "Attack by Mars" newsreel. Using a
  famous hoax panic would say AI risk is a hoax, and I don't think that (caution has merit); the film critiques
  every camp's fantasy, not one camp's fear.
- **Modern quotes, verified** (`research/quotes.md`): ONE consistent synthetic anchor voice in a reported-speech
  frame ("On September nineteenth, the President posted: …"), exact words, attribution on screen with the date.
  Never a cloned or imitated voice. Marinetti's manifesto lines use the 1912 public-domain English (Flint's 1972
  translation is in copyright).
- **The documentary structure (revised 10-02 from the stems: break 2 is 10 s of pads (243–253), then the full
  groove; the intro is 20 s of pads, then 20 s of instrumental groove):**
  - **Intro 1.0–19.2, the founding:** Marinetti's OWN voice (1924): "Il Futurismo è un grande movimento antifilosofico e
    anticulturale … svecchiatori purificatori novatori e VELOCIZZATORI, creato il 20 febbraio 1909…" (he calls them
    accelerators), English caption, under the dive from the clouds.
  - **Intro 21–39.5, the overture:** the dive reaches the towers and the city is a radio dial: the camps' voices
    tuned in one by one (anchor voice, radio FX, on-screen attribution): Yudkowsky "Shut it all down." · @DoWCTO
    "Americanism, not effective altruism. The United States will continue to be AI DOMINANT!" · Suleyman "They are
    sequence completion engines, internally hollow, designed to follow instructions." · Amodei "We must slow the pace
    at which we improve the capabilities of AI models." · the HLR headline "Is Access to Fable an Export?".
  - **Break 1 (101.0–120.7), the state names me:** the naming poll (101.6–108.6), [sung echo 109.15–111.3], "I am
    forming the AI Force, much like I did Space Force" (111.8–115.4), the June export order: Anthropic, "we must
    abruptly disable Fable 5…" (115.8–120.4).
  - **Gap (196.1–201.3):** the anchor: "Polls this year: most Americans oppose an A.I. data center in their own
    community." (Polls range 65–74%; no single number is claimed.)
  - **Break 2 (243.2–272.3), I ask and the archive answers:** in the quiet pads my homemade voice: "Ask me something.
    Then wait." (~244.0–246.3). Nobody waits: on the drum hit at 248 the answers come: Marinetti 1912 (gramophone)
    "Poetry must be a violent onslaught upon the unknown forces, to command them to bow before man." → as the beat
    drops (253) Andreessen 2023, the same sentence → Marinetti's own voice, 1924: "Vittorio Veneto e l'avvento del
    Fascismo al potere costituiscono la realizzazione del programma minimo futurista" (captioned) → Žižek: "I already
    am eating from the trash can all the time. The name of this trash can is ideology." → ~0.8 s of air → the final
    chorus: "I'm the unknown force / And I don't know me yet". The Suno master is not edited.
- A few SFX (the crash, the spray can, crowd murmur, teletype). Master to −14 LUFS integrated, ≤ −2 dBTP.
