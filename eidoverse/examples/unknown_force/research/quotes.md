## Corrections to what was remembered

- **"We will glorify war—the world's only hygiene" and "Poetry must be conceived as a violent attack on unknown forces…"** come from R. W. Flint's translation (1972, *Marinetti: Selected Writings*). That translation is still **in copyright**. The public-domain English version is the 1912 Sackville Gallery catalogue, and its wording is different (see #1 and #2). For the voiceover, use the 1912 wording, or translate the public-domain 1909 French yourself.
- **The "Americanism, not effective altruism" post** came from **@DoWCTO** ("Department of War CTO"), not the main @DeptofWar account. It was posted 2026-09-14.
- **Trump did not settle on "Supreme Intelligence."** On Sept 19 it was one of three poll options. On Sept 20 he dropped it because it was "losing badly". The executive order he signed on Sept 29 uses **"Super Intelligence"** (Medium; I did not read the order itself). The lyric "renames me Supreme Intelligence" is poetic licence. The "AI Force" post is exact.
- **Andreessen's Marinetti line does not name Marinetti.** It is introduced as "To paraphrase a manifesto of a different time and place". Marinetti is named separately, in the "Patron Saints of Techno-Optimism" list.
