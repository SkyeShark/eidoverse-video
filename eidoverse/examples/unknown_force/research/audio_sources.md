## The US rule used here

Under the Music Modernization Act (2018), pre-1972 sound recordings enter the US public domain on a sliding scale:

- published before 1923: public domain since 2022-01-01
- published 1923–1946: public domain 100 years after publication. So **1924 → PD on 2025-01-01**, 1925 → 2026, 1926 → 2027, and 1935 → 2036.
- published 1947–1956: 110 years
- published 1957–1972: until 2067

Italy (the country of origin for Marinetti and Russolo): phonogram rights lasted 50 years and expired long before the 2013 EU extension to 70 years, which did not revive them. The texts and compositions are PD as well: Marinetti died in 1944 and Antonio Russolo in 1942, both more than 70 years ago, and both were published before 1931.

## Checked and rejected (not US public domain)

- **Internet Archive "Great 78 Project" sound-effects discs (George Blood transfers).** These include Standard Sound Effect "AIRPLANE EFFECTS", "NEWS BROADCAST EFFECTS" (telegraph sounder, presses, newsboy) and Major "SIGNALS". The discs are undated, but they are radio-transcription libraries from the 1930s–50s, so they stay under US copyright until 2031–2067. The project is a preservation and lending collection, not a licence. Good to listen to for reference only. Examples: https://archive.org/details/78_news-broadcast-effects_gbia0467987a · https://archive.org/details/78_airplane-effects_gbia0467989b
- **The "russolo-crepitatore" item** (archive.org, described as a "1914 original recording"). No 1914 intonarumori recording is known to exist. This is almost certainly the Wergo CD's modern sample of a replica, so it is not PD.
- **The other Russolo tracks on the Wergo / Ubu CD** (Risveglio, Ululatore and so on) are modern performances on replicas (1977–78, Daniele Lombardi and others), so they are copyrighted.
- **Marinetti's *Sintesi musicali futuriste*** (Columbia, 1931): not US PD until 2032.
- **Cramps *Futura: Poesia Sonora*** (1976 recordings by Lora-Totino and others): copyrighted.

- The strongest "documentary" moment is **Marinetti's own 1924 voice** (*Definizione*) under the synthetic reading of the 1912 English "violent onslaught upon the unknown forces". Then **Russolo's 1920s intonarumori** run into the Andreessen paraphrase. Every archival layer there is real and public domain.
