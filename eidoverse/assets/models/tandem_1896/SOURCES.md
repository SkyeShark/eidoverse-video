## Original work

`tandem.glb` was made by Claude (Opus 5.5) with Skye for the DAISY music
video (2026-09): every part of its geometry, UVs, layered look-development,
bakes, pivots and layout extras. So were `eidoverse/props/tandem.js`, with its
chain, spoke-blur, rider-IK and teapot code, and the build scripts in
[`../tandem_1896_src/`](../tandem_1896_src/README.md). They are released with
this repository (see [LICENSE](../../../../LICENSE) and
[CREDITS](../../../../CREDITS.md)). No third-party mesh data is included.
The stoker is three's `TeapotGeometry`, the Utah teapot (Martin Newell, 1975),
built at runtime.

The design is a period pattern, not a copy of one maker's machine. It follows
the 1890s lugged safety tandem: a Swift-style stoker bar clamped to the
captain's seat pin, a fixed-wheel timing chain between the cranks and a drive
chain to the rear wheel.

## Period references (provenance only)

The builder modelled from photographs of four machines: the 1896 Swift Tandem
Safety, the 1896/97 Columbia racing tandem, an 1895 Rambler and a 1900 Royal
Enfield. The source addresses of those photos were not recorded, so they are
named here by subject only. The photos are **not** in the repository and are
not part of any texture.
