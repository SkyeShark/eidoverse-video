# book_sfx — provenance

All clips are cut from CC0 (Creative Commons Zero / public domain) recordings; no attribution is
required by the licence, but the sources are recorded here so the cuts can be re-made or swapped.
Processing: cut to the event, 60–80 Hz high-pass (handling rumble), short fades, level matched by
the active-part RMS (turn/fan ≈ −27 dBFS, open ≈ −24, close ≈ −22 dBFS, peaks ≤ −1 dBFS), mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit.
