The label is original wording rasterized locally using the installed Windows
Bahnschrift font. No font file is redistributed. Hard Ops, DECALmachine and
Cablerator are installed authoring tools, not included dependencies of the GLB.
Their code, proprietary libraries and example decal assets are not distributed.
No robot manufacturer's meshes or Isaac Sim assets are included.

Manufacturer photos and documents were inspection references in the separate
authoring project. Those references and their authoring manifests are excluded
from this public asset package; there is no bundled `references/` directory.
Do not redistribute reference imagery as part of the kit's freely licensed content.

The offline inspector includes Three.js r184 (MIT); its license is in
[the vendor license](../../robotics/inspector/vendor/three/LICENSE). The local
Pillow dependency (HPND license) and review tool dependencies retain their
own licenses and are excluded from the model package.
